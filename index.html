What EthiCook does

📷

Scan and forget

Point the camera at a product — EthiCook reads the barcode and the printed expiry date at once and saves the item automatically. No typing.

🥬

Fresh food too

No barcode? Pick the food from a list and we suggest how many days it keeps — adjustable.

🔔

Never get surprised

A colour-coded pantry sorted by what expires first, a two-week calendar, gentle reminders, and a home-screen widget.

🍳

Cook it, don't bin it

200 recipe ideas, built into the app and ranked by the ingredients you already have — no internet needed.

🌍

Help Open Food Facts

Found a product the database doesn't know? You can add it to Open Food Facts right from the app — entirely optional, with your own free account.

Made to reduce waste

Throwing away forgotten food is throwing away money — and it's hard on the planet. EthiCook tracks your food and its dates so nothing gets lost at the back of the fridge. Product data comes from Open Food Facts under the Open Database License. Born from an idea by Giovanni Binda.
